ResearchGap AI

Research-support workspace

A local-first workspace that helps you explore candidate research gaps across a collection of academic papers you upload. It combines PDF ingestion, semantic retrieval and AI-assisted synthesis in one interface.

  • JWT sign-in with protected workspace access.
  • PDFs are embedded locally and searched with PostgreSQL and pgvector.
  • Candidate gaps are grounded in the uploaded papers, with saved history and downloadable reports.
  • Separate Overview, Paper Library, Gap Analysis and History screens in a dark interface.

Gaps are candidate findings, not proof of novelty, and need human verification. Scanned or image-only PDFs (OCR) are not supported.

  • FastAPI
  • PostgreSQL
  • pgvector
  • Sentence Transformers
  • Docker
  • JWT

SonicMiner AI

Audio feature extraction and analysis

An interactive platform for uploading audio files, inspecting them one by one or in batches, comparing how similar they are, and exporting the features as structured data for machine-learning experiments.

  • Extracts 39 features per file: six core spectral and energy measures, 13 MFCC, 12 Chroma, 7 Spectral Contrast and tempo, plus duration and sample rate.
  • Shows waveforms and MFCC, Chroma and Spectral Contrast heatmaps.
  • Exports CSV for all files, selected files, selected features, or a single result.
  • Compares one song against many, or any two files, using their feature representations.
  • Optional Gemini explanations of what the measurements mean.
  • Python
  • Librosa
  • NumPy
  • Pandas
  • Matplotlib
  • Google Gemini

Inkwell

MERN stack blogging platform

Anyone can browse public posts; registered users can write, edit and delete their own. Server-side ownership checks and authentication protect user-specific actions.

  • Registration and login with JWT; passwords hashed with bcrypt.
  • Time-limited, hashed password-reset tokens.
  • Edit and delete limited to the post's owner on the server.
  • Title and tag search with pagination.
  • REST API split into routes, controllers, models and middleware; React app with public and protected routes.

Planned next: comments, a rich-text editor and direct image uploads.
